# Problems found in the M.P.s themselves

While writing study cards from every Volume I and II M.P. (Nov 2026), these issues turned up in the city's published text. Cards follow each M.P. as written; none of the conflicting numbers below are tested. Worth sending to whoever maintains the M.P.s.

## Vol I: Conduct, Uniforms & General Policy (100–102)

- M.P. 102.01 (Rules of Conduct) is referenced by 102.02 and 102.05 but is not on the city's policies page, so the app can't cover it yet. M.P. 102.02A (detailed Class A wear standards) is also referenced but absent.
- M.P. 102.05C has overlapping thresholds: one section says 'UNDER $200' / '$200 or less' and the next says '$200 AND OVER'. Cards use '$200 or less' vs 'over $200' as the staff-work section states. The MP (04/06) also references Performance Auditing and Form 90-68D, which may be outdated given 102.05B's OSI process.
- M.P. 102.05B defines NOI as 'Notice of Inquiry' but Addendum A calls it 'Notice of Investigation'. Personnel Rule '2lBl6' appears garbled in the text (likely 21B16); not used on cards.
- M.P. 102.12 (06/19) references the Domino/Lotus Notes email system and AOL/Yahoo/MSN/KaZaA examples; likely outdated. Its PRIVACY AND MONITORING section is duplicated in the text.
- M.P. 102.14 has a review date of 05/2021 and lists named individual contacts (phone numbers) that may be stale; not used on cards.
- M.P. 102.05 and 102.07A show Date Revised 11/2026.

## Vol I: Leave, Shift Trades & Hours (103) / Vol I: Overtime, Assignments & Staffing (104)

- 103.01: vacation accrual table row for 20+ years is garbled in the PDF text ('20 yrs. 1 mo. To 15 21 ...'); no card uses the accrual table.
- 103.04: internal conflict - Definition says LTD is 66-2/3 percent of basic monthly salary, but Amount says 50 percent. No card tests the percentage.
- 103.07 vs 104.03B: 103.07 says being unable to work an ERP day because of the max-hours rule 'will result in an ERP refusal', while 104.03B says such a day 'will be reassigned based on organizational needs'. No card tests this point.
- 103.02C: text contains leftover parenthetical/strikethrough fragments (e.g., '(within five (5) days after receipt of a letter from the) before returning'); cards use only the clear 30-day rules.
- 103.02F: MP title is 'Alternate Assignment/Industrial Review Committee', but 103.02E and 104.02B call it the 'Transitional Work (Assignment) Review Committee'. 103.02B revision date reads '0/24'.
- 104.03 (rev. 09/09) and 104.01/104.01A/104.02D (2008-2014) are old revisions; 104.02C and 104.02G cite details 'as described in M.P. 104.02' (form 90-81D, CE requirements) that 104.02 does not contain.
- 104.02A is mostly staff job descriptions; cards cover the shift-change formula, interview tiebreak, and a few position requirements only.

## Vol I: Member Services, Wellness & Recognition (105)

- 105.01: The Tier table text is incomplete in the PDF extraction. The Tier 1/Tier 2 blood sugar and HbA1c values and the Body Fat % row are missing, so no cards were written on those values.
- 105.01A: A '*8 Function of Crisis Care Flow Chart' is referenced but is an image and did not extract.
- 105.02 vs 105.02E: 105.02 says the LODD I Honor Watch runs 'from death to burial/interment', but 105.02E says Honor Watch sign-up runs 'from time of death until the start of the funeral'. The cards use each MP's own wording, and no card asks for the end point in a way that conflicts.
- 105.03: The Local 493 'Last Check Meeting' timing conflicts: section 6 says 6-12 months before retirement, and Addendum A says within 3 months. The City Retirement Office address also differs (200 vs 201 W. Washington). No cards were written on these points.
- 105.02C (04/06) uses old titles (South Deputy, Good and Welfare Officer, Assistant Chief of Personnel). 105.01C, 105.02A, 105.02B and 105.10 are dated 11/08; 105.09 is dated 04/06. These may be outdated.
- 105.11: The decision-maker for petitions differs within the MP: the Deputy Chief of HR for non-disciplinary documents, and the Personnel Officer/Chief of Personnel Services for reprimands. Cards avoid this point.
- 105.06 lists an old phone number (495-7802) and position titles that may no longer exist (Personnel Control Officer, Management Services Administrator).

## Vol I: Media, Stations & Public Contact (106–107) / Vol I: Safety Programs, Reporting & Records (110–111, 208)

- 107.02A: file is named 107.02A but the MP header reads 'M.P. 107.02' (revised 07/24); cards use src 'M.P. 107.02A' to match the file list, question and why text use 107.02A too.
- 110.01: file title is 'Vehicle Accident Reporting' but the MP is titled 'Vehicle Collision Reporting' (rev. 03/2025).
- 110.02: source filename misspelled 'Inury Reporting'; text is fine.
- 110.07 and 110.16 are dated 5/22 and 107.03 / 111.03 are dated 09/18, past the 5-year review cycle in 208.01; 208.01 itself is dated 12/18.
- 110.16: the third mandatory hearing-protection condition reads oddly ('STS of 10 dB or greater... when the 2, 3, 4K average is above 25 dB from baseline'); not carded.
- 110.18: cites 'ARS 13-1204.8.C' for elevation to aggravated assault, which looks like a typo; card avoids the statute number. MP has no revision date beyond 6/2020.
- 110.19 tells members to see 'MP 102.02 Uniforms, Insignias and Hair Regulations'; not verified against the index.
- 110.20 (rev. 08/2026) and 111.04 (rev. 10/2026) are very recent revisions.
- 106.09 says requests are approved by the Assistant Fire Chief over Public Affairs, but the attached form 95-102 shows 'Division Head Approval'.
- Part 2 of the 110–111/208 topic was split because the combined topic had 64 cards.

## Vol II: Command, Mayday & Sectors (201)

- 201.05B file is titled 'Support Sector' but the body text calls it 'Resource Sector' throughout (RM-50 = Resource Officer); cards say 'Resource (Support) Sector'.
- 201.05G and 201.08 read as dated (slide film, transparencies, department mail, 'a new form has been developed'); cards avoid those details.
- 201.08 is mostly blank evaluation forms; only page 1 has procedure text. 201.07's final landing-zone diagram and hand-signal pages did not extract usefully and were not used.

## Vol II: Fireground Support, Loss Control & Overhaul (202) / Vol II: EMS Sectors & Multi-Casualty (203)

- M.P. 202.12D (Positive Pressure Ventilation) has no usable text: the file only says "This SOP is currently out for review." No cards written.
- M.P. 203.01 (03/00-R) defines a multi-patient incident as "fewer than twenty (25) patients" (word and numeral conflict). No card tests that number; MCI (25-100) and disaster (100+) are tested.
- M.P. 203.01, 203.03 and 203.04 (all 03/00) use IMMEDIATE/DELAYED/MINOR categories, S.T.A.R.T./Central Arizona triage and triage tags, while M.P. 203.02 (05/26-R) uses RAMP with Immediate/Delayed/Dead-Dying/INA categories and triage tape. The older MPs look outdated relative to 203.02; cards cite each MP as written.
- M.P. 203.02 page 3 is titled "RAMP Triage" but has no extractable text (probably a flowchart image), so the RAMP algorithm itself isn't carded.
- M.P. 203.01 says the Triage Report is radioed "to Alarm" in one place and to Command elsewhere; 203.02 says to Command.
- M.P. 203.06A (10/04-N) is written as a proposal ("would respond", "would be assigned"); cards treat its stated assignments as policy.
- M.P. 202.12 and 202.12B both cover securing the scene (plastic over vent holes/windows, remove glass shards); carded only once each on other points to avoid duplicates.

## Vol II: Hazmat, Radiological & WMD (204, 207) / Vol II: Technical Rescue (204.08–204.13)

- 204.01 cites 'Command Procedures; MP 204.01' for the Strategic Decision-Making Model, a self-reference (likely should point to the Command Procedures MP).
- 204.08-204.13 are dated 07/11-R; 204.11 lists 'Car 957 South or North' as Safety Officer/Technical Advisor options in Phase I, but Additional Considerations lists 'Regional Special Operations qualified Safety Officers' instead; no card tests either list.
- 204.03 and 204.03A read as old (references Maricopa County Civil Defense, Arizona Radiation Regulatory Agency, 'MR/hr' units); cards use the numbers as written (2 mR/hr, 2,000 ft).
- 204.06 says the control log goes to the 'Special Operations Sector' but elsewhere 'Special Operations Section'.
- 207.01A references 'MP 204.14C?' (with a question mark) and M.P. 201.14D for decon/treatment protocols; those numbers don't match current MPs (decon lines are actually in 207.01B, treatment in 207.01C). It also says 'Amyl Nitrate' where 207.01C says amyl nitrite.
- 207.01B says the personnel decon setup is optimally run by six technicians, then the diagram note says five; 'Victorian instrument' is a typo for Victoreen. Not carded.
- 207.01C is a physician-level treatment protocol (it states some drugs/procedures are outside paramedic scope) and has apparent errors: nitrites 'produce orthostatic hypertension' (likely hypotension), child thiosulfate dose '0.4 mg/kg' conflicts with 1.65 ml/kg. Cards only test field-level facts (chlorine density/observation, mustard onset, cyanide mild exposure, adult atropine 2 mg, nerve vapor onset).

## Vol II: Dispatch, Staging & Driving (205.02–205.11)

- 205.02 (10/97) numbers two different list items '22' (auto accidents and 'any other medical emergency'); the second is presumably 24.
- 205.03 and 205.05A (10/97), 205.04 (03/03), 205.04A (07/99), 205.05B (05/95), 205.07 and 205.11 (01/95) are old revisions; dispatch names (Channel 1, DS2, TRO) and the 205.04 list of contract cities may be out of date.
- 205.03 gives 2-1 Medical as 'Level 1 and 2 hospitals' but 1st Alarm Medical as 'Level 1 hospitals'; carded as written.
- 205.05 location illustrations, 205.05A org chart and 205.07A figures 1–8 are images and did not extract; cards use the text only.

## Vol II: Special Responses — Airports, Freeway, Power & Solar (205.13–205.24)

- 205.13 text has garbled gate numbers ('Gate 118 117', 'Gate 200 F') and page 7 is blank; no cards written on gate numbers.
- 205.13 Alert 1/Alert 2 definitions for twin-engine general aviation aircraft (under/over 12,500 lbs) read awkwardly; cards avoid that detail.
- 205.13C (rev 12/12) names a specific airport manager and personal phone numbers, which are likely outdated; not carded.
- 205.15 is dated 12/98 and uses old names (e.g., 'Squaw Peak' for SR-51, 'Mini-Stack' discouraged); glossary cards stick to the Stack/lane numbering.
- 205.15A says the Bus Transit Tunnel 'will not be in service for some time'; cards still test its use as the MP directs.
- 205.16 (09/00) is old (references MAST suits, 'District Van-5' radio ID); the program may no longer exist.
- 205.19 (03/00) lists the Water Dept number as 261-8000 with no area code; not carded.
- 205.20A file name misspells 'Energy' ('Evergy'). 205.20A and 205.20B repeat the same residential BESS steps (40 ft isolation, 12-hr monitoring, 10-min temp readings); cards split between them to avoid duplicates.
- 205.22 says the SERT 'shall be compromised of' (should read 'comprised of').
- 205.24 appendices B and D (Contingency Plan Checklist, SGI form, NOTAM filing) are blank in the extracted text; page 13's VO description says 'equally responsible' and leaves the team-communications line unfinished ('(team? Pilot?)').

## Vol II: Special Operations & Hazards (206)

- M.P. 206.05 (05/93) and 206.13 (05/93) are very old; 206.13 refers to the 'Hurst Tool' by brand name.
- M.P. 206.08 (01/04) repeats the 'first unit arriving... initial report' heading twice with different bullet lists; cards treat both lists as one report. It also says Command should assign a company to 'assist Foam 44' in the Foam Sector though most foam ops reference Foam 34, and it cites confined space procedures as 'M.P. 204.8'.
- M.P. 206.21: OCS is listed as 750-950 V DC while the TPSS output is listed as 650-950 V DC; cards use the OCS figure (750-950).
- M.P. 206.06 cites NFPA 1851 2014 Edition; M.P. 206.16A (07/03) is old (bio-exposure card).
- M.P. 206.09 and 206.09A are near-duplicates (C958 liaison, 1/4-mile premise alert, Code 2, 2-hour rotation); cards were split so no question repeats across them.
