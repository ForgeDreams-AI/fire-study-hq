# Content changes — November 2026

Every card in the app was reviewed. This file lists what changed so an instructor can check it.

## Across every card

- **Answer giveaway removed.** The correct option used to be the longest one on 85% of cards, and in Recall mode it was always listed first. Now all four options are about the same length (the correct one is longest on ~21% of cards, close to chance) and Recall mode shuffles them.
- **Wrong options rewritten** on all cards so they are believable mistakes (real misconceptions, near-miss numbers, wrong order) instead of joke answers.
- **Every Paramedic card now has an explanation** (145 had none).
- Card positions are unchanged, so saved progress still lines up. Duplicate cards were replaced in place with new questions.

## Factual and doctrine fixes

### Fire Dynamics

- **1.2** — Removed 'steam push' — FSRI found properly applied exterior water does not push fire/steam onto occupants; consistent with 2.36.
- **1.6** — Stem made unambiguous for backdraft (puffing, smoke drawn back in, sealed hot compartment, air inrush).
- **1.8** — Water extinguishes primarily by cooling (heat), not by breaking the chain reaction.
- **1.9** — Single opening is bidirectional (intake low, exhaust high); answer and distractors corrected.
- **1.10** — Stopped calling the smoke-layer interface the 'neutral plane'; they are distinct concepts.
- **1.11** — Changed 'water first, then vent — every time' to coordinated vent with water, consistent with concepts/2.1/2.15.
- **1.13** — FSRI finding is fastest water from either location; no longer presents exterior-first as the only correct answer.
- **1.17** — Supermarket refrigerants are mostly A1 HFC/HFO, CO2 or A3 propane (not primarily A2L) and largely odorless; removed smell cue.
