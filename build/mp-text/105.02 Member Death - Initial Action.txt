PURPOSE
The Phoenix Fire Department and Local 493 Member Services work together to ensure that fallen
firefighters receive a fitting tribute, prioritizing the family's wishes and coordinating with the
funeral director, honor guard, and other involved parties. Responsibilities include identifying and
meeting the needs of both the family and firefighting crews, providing emotional and spiritual
support, and maintaining clear communication and organization. The response involves multiple
elements, such as initiating an Incident Management Team (IMT), managing ceremonial details,
and overseeing logistics for memorial services, honor watch, and processions. The department
also ensures ongoing support for the family, including benefits assistance and remembrance
events.

Key roles include the Member Service Representatives from the Local 493 and the Phoenix Fire
Department, Family Liaison, and Honor Guard, who coordinate efforts from initial notification
through funeral services and long-term follow-up. The Fire Chief oversees notifications, unified
command, and ceremonial preparations, while department-wide communications ensure
personnel are informed and able to participate in honoring the fallen member. Honor Watch (only
for LODD I) ensures the firefighter is never left alone from the time of death to interment.
Additional responsibilities involve securing the member’s personal effects, providing security at
the family’s home, and coordinating with local law enforcement. Follow-up care continues
beyond the funeral, including benefits processing, memorial ceremonies, and ongoing family
support. This MP has been designed so that it works in concert with, and does not conflict with,
the criteria of the International Association of Fire Fighters (IAFF), the National Fallen Fire
Fighter Foundation and Memorial (NFFFF), Public Safety Officer Benefits (PSOB), et al.

Overarching Objectives

Although each organizational element of a Member Death Response has objectives specific to
their function, all elements and functions will operate under the premise of these overarching
objectives:
   1. Conduct the Member Death response (initial actions, honor watch, funeral,
       interment, etc.)utilizing a NIMS/ICS-compliant, Unified Command organization,
       organizational chart, organizational objectives and planning checklists.
   2. Plan to help maximize the welfare of those assigned to and attending the series of

Organizational and Operational Plan

1. Initiate Unified Command immediately to ensure a strong connection with those we
   coordinateservices with, especially Phoenix Police. A line of duty death may require three
   IMT/ICS organizations under a UnifiedCommand for:
              1) Resolving the original incident in which the member passed away.
              2) The death investigation.
              3) The memorial-related services.
2. Use PFD and PPD Operations and other personnel in the related ICS/NIMS
   positions/functions to take advantage of those inherent management designs, skills and
   abilities, while adding thosespecialty positions that are necessary.
3. Conduct regular and structured planning meetings and operational briefings and use all
   othertools in the NIMS ICS system.
MEMBER DEATH CATEGORIES WITH MATRIX
There are currently four categories of member deaths in order to help us to plan our initial
response,funerals and recognition programs. LODD I, LODD II, Off Duty Death of Active
Member, and Retiree Death.
This is a guide for internal use only (Honor Guard, Executive Staff, L493) this is because
services cannot always be guaranteed, they are based on multiple factors, including
staffing, logistics, equipment, etc.
L493 Member Services contacts the Honor Guard on behalf of the family to request
general or specific services. The event commander works with Member services and,
when appropriate, the family to discuss any specialized services desired or offered.

1. Line of Duty Death I (LODD I)

   This is when a member died as result of, or during the performance of their duties,
   operating on, at, enroute to, or travelling in connection with, a call for service, training,
   firefighting, EMS, search, rescue, recovery, lifesaving, etc. This may include an act while
   off duty, such as a life-saving attempt.

   A. Honors Given:
          • Honor Watch: From death to burial (maintained by Member Services, L493).
          • Full Honor Guard presence for Honors to member at hospital.
          • Receiving member at M.E. Office, or hospital. Flag draped over the member, full honors
             started.

   B. Visitation, ME, mortuary services:
         • Casket draped with U.S. Flag.
         • Member escorted to/from Mortuary/ME office, including apparatus, PD escort.
         • Honor Watch, started and maintained by member services, L493.
         • Full Honor Guard escort to/from any viewing or mortuary services needed prior to

2. Line of Duty Death II (LODD II)

   This is when an active sworn member of this department has suffered an injury or illness
   while performing any of their duties. This member experienced an injury or illness and
   had a delay in their passing; or an injury that was recovered from but later lead to a
   member’s death, any occupation illness deemed “line of duty” by L493 and the City of
   Phoenix. A member given Accidental Disability retirement and dies of the disability later.
   Presumptive Cardiacs, Cancers, etc. (Official cause of death will be determined by the
   Maricopa County Medical Examiner’s Office). Other On-Duty Deaths include:
   Commuting as defined under COP rules (2 hours to and from work). Honor Guard/Pipes &
   Drums member death on event/business. Death that has not been definitively determined to
   be caused by work. Not on-duty with PFD, but on Union business or event.

   A. Honors Given:
         • No Honor Watch
         • Full Honor Guard presence for Honors to member at hospital.

3. Off-Duty Death of an active duty (current) member

   This is when a sworn member of this department has suffered an injury or illness while off
   duty that led to their death. (Official cause of death will be determined by the Maricopa
   County Medical Examiner’s office)

   A. Honors Given:
        • No Honor Watch
        • Honor Guard presence for Honors to member at hospital/home.

             Honors started.
         • Member escorted to Mortuary/ME office, by Honor Guard
         • US Flag from member will be flown at members Fire Station or wherever appropriate for
             no less than 2 days. Recovered, folded and returned to member.
