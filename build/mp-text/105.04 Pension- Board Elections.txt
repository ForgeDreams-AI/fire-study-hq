                            PENSION/BOARD ELECTIONS

 Related Policies:

BACKGROUND

All sworn active Phoenix Fire Department members of any rank who are participants in the
Arizona Public Safety Personnel Retirement System (PSPRS) as defined under Title 38, Article
4, Chapter 5 of the Arizona Revised Statute may serve as sworn member of the Phoenix Fire
Pension Board. The five-member board is comprised of the Mayor or their designee; The Chair
of the Civil Service Board; A citizen representative and two sworn members elected by secret
ballot to four-year staggard terms. The Phoenix Fire Pension Board is an administrative arm of
the PSPRS and is responsible for determining Line of Duty Death Survivor Benefits and
Disability Benefits for members.

POLICY

When a vacancy occurs, or a term of one of the sworn members expires on the Firefighter's
Pension Board, the Fire Chief or an assigned designee shall hold nominations and an election for
that vacancy. These nominations andelections shall be governed by this procedure. If a sworn
incumbent member’s term is expiring and the incumbent intends to run for re-election,
notification of the incumbent’s intent will be reported to all members when the nomination
process is opened and published. The incumbent will need to be nominated as described below to
qualify for the election

NOMINATIONS

Any active Department member may nominate themselves or any other active member
of theDepartment of any rank who is a member of the PSPRS.
Members wishing to submit a name in nomination for the Pension Board shall make notification
to the Administrative Office of the Fire Chief or its assigned designee. Nominated members
should actively contact any fire station or Fire Department work area to communicate their
desire to occupythe office they seek. This campaigning must be done during the campaigner's
off-duty hours.
. If only one nomination is received, a single voting location will be designated and published
with the location of the ballot box and the dates for voting (Shifts A, B, C) . If more than one
nomination is received, a notice will be published with the dates for voting (Shifts A, B, C) and
Battalion Chiefs accompanied by a Union Officer will visit each station on each shift with a
locked ballot box for which members can vote.
All names received by the Fire Chief's Office or its assigned designee will be printed

alphabetically on a nominating list and attached to the ballot box. A member may only vote for
the names that are listed on the nominating list. Absolutely No write-ins will be counted and the
ballot listing the write-in will be discarded after the counting of the other ballots. As each
member votes, they will log their name onto a sign-in sheet. This will provide for a count and
record of members voting. Thiscount will be compared to the ballots cast upon completion.
After voting has closed the ballots will be counted in the presence of members of the Fire
Chief'sstaff or its designee and Local #493.
If any member receives 50% plus one vote of all the nominating ballots cast, that member shall be
declared the winner and another election need not be held.

RUN OFF ELECTION

If no member receives a majority of 50 % plus one vote of all nominations cast, a run-off
election will be held between the two candidates receiving the most votes.. If tied, selection
will be determined by a coin toss.
The completed ballots shall be destroyed no sooner than 90 days from the certification of the
electionresults by the Fire Chiefs staff/its designee and Local #493.
