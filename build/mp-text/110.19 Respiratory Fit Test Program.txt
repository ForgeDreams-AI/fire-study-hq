                       RESPIRATORY FIT TEST PROGRAM

 M.P. 110.19                                              Date Revised: 04/26
 This policy is for internal use only and does not expand an employee’s legal duty or civil liability in any way. This
 policy should not be construed as creating a duty to act or a higher duty of care with respect to third-party civil claims
 against employees, the Phoenix Fire Department (PFD) or the City of Phoenix. A violation of this policy, if proven,
 can only form the basis for non-judicial administrative action by the employer in accordance with the laws and rules
 governing employee discipline.

 Related Policies: M.P. 202.02, M.P. 202.05B, OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134

Purpose
It is the policy of the Phoenix Fire Department to provide its members with appropriate
respiratory protection to safeguard them from injury during Fire Department activities. This
protection must align with the diverse services the Department provides and comply with
applicable OSHA standards.
This program establishes the standards and procedures for respiratory protection, ensuring the
highest level of safety for our members through proper testing and compliance.
Scope
There are many different types of protection used within the various sections within the Phoenix
Fire Department as members respond to a variety of environments requiring different levels and
types of protection. This program details the standards and methods used to ensure the highest
level of safety for our members and ensure compliance with OSHA standards for respiratory
protection in accordance with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134.
Frequency
All members working in a position requiring respiratory protection as defined by OSHA 29 CFR
1910.134 must be tested for proper fit:
    -   Before using respiratory protection for the first time
    -   At least once annually
    -   When returning from transitional work assignment
    -   If out of the field due to injury, illness, or any other leave during the annual fit testing
        process
    -   After any changes that could affect the fit of respiratory protection including, but not
        limited to, facial scarring, dental changes, cosmetic surgery, or an obvious change in body
        weight
    -   If member identifies leakage in mask or can smell vapor or fumes while wearing
        respiratory protection
    -   Anytime a member has concerns about the fit of their respiratory protection

Program Roles and Responsibilities
Department Safety Officer
The Department Safety Officer (DSO) is the Deputy Chief of Safety and Prevention and acts as
the director of the respiratory protection program. The DSO reports to the Executive Team.
Responsibilities of the DSO include:
           o   Provides direction and oversight to the Program Manager
           o   Develop, implement, and evaluate the Respiratory Protection Program
           o   Ensures compliance with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134 standards
           o   Provides direction and oversight to the Program Manager
           o   Assist with identifying the task or environment requiring the use of a respirator
           o   Recommend appropriate respirators and filtering media for tasks that require the
               use of a respirator
Respiratory Protection Program Manager
The program manager is a Safety Officer and is the point of contact for program delivery. The
Program manager reports to the DSO and is responsible to:
           o Oversee operations of the annual Respiratory Fit Test Program
           o Ensure members are tracked for completion throughout the annual respiratory fit
             test program
           o Reviews and sends communication provided by Program Coordinators prior to
             and during the annual respiratory fit test program to ensure compliance of all
             members. Communication includes but is not limited to bulletins, emails,
             schedule information, and any pertinent changes to the program
           o Ensure testing site locations have been reserved by program coordinators
           o Coordinate with the command training center to schedule all field units and staff
             members needing fit testing
           o Provide resource with a report of the total number of each size SCBA mask and
             mesh distributed during annual fit testing as well as the total number of each size
             SCBA mask used by members
           o Oversee the process of ensuring all fit test machines are calibrated annually. This
             includes acquiring a quote from the vendor, ensuring the quote has been
             approved, and work to coordinate between program coordinators and resource
             management to ensure machines needing calibrated are sent appropriately
           o Ensures efficiency in the program and works with the DSO to ensure budget is
             properly managed
           o Evaluate the effectiveness of the program
Program Coordinators
The program coordinators implement and oversee the delivery of the Respiratory Fit Test
Program. Program coordinators report to the Program Manager and are responsible to:
           o Ensure all equipment is in proper operating condition
           o Track for completion of all tested members

Test Facilitators
Test facilitators are scheduled by Program Coordinators to deliver the respiratory fit test to all
members in the field. All fit test facilitators will be knowledgeable and proficient in the use of
quantitative and qualitative fit testing equipment and processes and documentation. Additionally,
test facilitators will ensure they are proficient in the setup, cleaning and storage of fit test
equipment, understand the different types of respirators and their respective testing procedures
and the limitations and capabilities of each.
Operations Member Roles and Responsibilities
District Commanders
District Commanders will receive compliance updates throughout the fit testing process and will
work with Battalion Chiefs to ensure all members are aware and are tested during the annual
testing period. In addition, District Commanders are also responsible for removing members
from Operations who are not compliant with their fit testing. Members will be pulled from
Operations and placed on their own leave until back in compliance and cleared through the
Safety Section.
Battalion/Division Chiefs
Battalion Chiefs will receive compliance updates and ensure all members in their
battalion/section complete fit testing during the testing period. Battalion/Division Chiefs will
communicate with District Commanders if there is a non-compliance or scheduling issue to
ensure all the members within their battalion/section are compliant.
Company Officers
Company Officers are responsible for ensuring their crew shows up when scheduled for fit
testing. Additionally, Company Officers will ensure any members who are off duty on the
scheduled day attend another session prior to the completion of the scheduled program.
As direct supervisors, the Company Officer is required to ensure no member utilizes any tight-
fitting respirator if they have facial hair that comes between the sealing surface of the facepiece
and the face or that interferes with valve function (ref. OSHA 29 CFR 1910.134(g)(1)(i)(A))

All Members
All members are personally responsible for their compliance with respiratory protection fit test
standards. Members whose duties require respiratory protection shall:
           o Ensure the required annual physical is complete annually
           o Attend their scheduled fit testing session
           o Ensure there is no facial hair at points where SCBA face-piece is designed to seal
             with the face. (Refer to MP 102.02 Uniforms, Insignias and Hair Regulations)
           o If unable to attend on the day scheduled, attend another session during the
             program period to ensure annual compliance.
           o If unable to attend anytime throughout the duration of annual fit testing, the
             member must coordinate with the Safety Section to be tested prior to returning to
             work.
           o Members will be pulled from Operations and placed on their own leave while out
             of compliance.
If significant physical changes happen such as facial surgery, weight change, facial scars, that
may affect the proper fit of a mask, members must request a fit test from the Safety Section to
ensure proper seal of all respiratory protection.
Documentation
Any member requiring the use of tight-fitting respiratory protection will be required to be
medically evaluated prior to face-piece fit testing. Documentation for qualitative and quantitative
testing for all members must be kept until the next annual test is completed.
Documentation should be stored in a secure database to ensure safety of the information.
Understanding of OSHA Standards
The Phoenix Fire Department fit testing program is based on and will remain in compliance with
OSHA’s respiratory protection standard, 29 CFR 1910.134, including the requirements for fit
testing, types of tests, and recordkeeping. Facilitators must be familiar with the requirements of
29 CFR 1910.134.
