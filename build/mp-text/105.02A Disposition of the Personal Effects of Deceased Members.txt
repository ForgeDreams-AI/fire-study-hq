                          PHOENIX FIRE DEPARTMENT

                           VOLUME 1 – Operations Manual

  DISPOSITION OF THE PERSONAL EFFECTS OF DECEASED MEMBERS

                                MP105.02A       11/08 – R

PURPOSE

The purpose of this procedure is to ensure the security and proper disposition of the
personal effects of a member in the event of his/her death. Personal effects shall include
all property owned by the deceased member that is on Fire Department property at the
time of the member's death.

RESPONSIBILITIES

In the event of the death of a member, whether on or off duty, the following steps shall be
followed in the gathering and disposition of his/her personal effects:

       The Performance Auditing Section will be assigned to secure the personal effects
       of the deceased member and deliver them to Fire Administration.

       The deceased member's vehicle will be secured at the worksite and the keys
       forwarded with the personal effects.

       The Personnel Section will conduct an inventory of the personal effects upon
       receipt and make a written record of all items.

                   •   The next of kin will be given priority consideration to receive the
                       property.
                   •   Care should be exercised in selecting a tactful time for delivery of
                       the personal effects to the next of kin.
                   •   Documentation is required indicating the date, time and location of
                       disposition. The name and the relationship of the individual
                       accepting the property will also be noted. Documentation will then
                       be forwarded to the Personnel Section for placement in the
                       deceased Member's personnel file.
