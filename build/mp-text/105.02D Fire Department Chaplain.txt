BACKGROUND
The Phoenix Fire Department Chaplain Program exists to provide emotional, spiritual, faith-
based, and practical support to our firefighters, their families, and the citizens we serve.
Chaplains respond to critical incidents, provide comfort during tragedy, and promote the overall
well-being of our members through presence, compassion, and connection.

STRUCTURE
Lead Chaplain
A designated Lead Chaplain, or Chaplain 1, will serve as the program manager and primary
point of contact between the Chaplain Program and the PFD. Sworn personnel shall not be
considered for this role.


RESPONSIBILITIES:
    •   Overseeing the overall function and direction of the program
    •   Coordinating scheduling and assignment of additional Chaplains
    •   Managing on-call coverage to provide Chaplain services
    •   Assisting in the selection, orientation, and mentorship of new Chaplains
    •   Acting as PFD’s official representative for matters involving chaplaincy


BACKUP CHAPLAINS
The Lead Chaplain will work with administration to appoint Backup Chaplains to assist with the
duties expected of the Chaplain. Backup Chaplains shall assist with the needs of the program and
be part of the succession plan for the future Lead Chaplain when needed.
Each Chaplain will have the same access to respond to incidents, provide member support, and
represent the department at events. However, this presence shall be in coordination with the lead
Chaplain to ensure consistency of the program.

APPOINTMENT
The Fire Department Chaplain shall be appointed by the Fire Chief after consultation with the
Firefighters’ Union President. The Chaplain will be considered part of the PFD Executive Team,
but will report to Chief of Member Services
Chaplains will serve as official City of Phoenix volunteers.
The Department Will Provide
   •   Uniform as defined in uniform policy
   •   City email addresses
   •   ID card
   •   Access to appropriate software and IT
   •   Computer
   •   Vehicle when resources are available


Operational support, including coordination and oversight, will be housed under the Division of
Member Services.
