PURPOSE
To establish a standardized, department-wide process for using the City of Phoenix PHXYou Learning
Management System to document, maintain, monitor, and audit training, continuing education (CE),
certifications, and other required training records for Phoenix Fire Department functional training
programs.

POLICY
PHXYou shall serve as the primary Department system for maintaining applicable training,
certification, and continuing education records. Functional sections shall continue to administer their
respective training programs and are responsible for maintaining the accuracy and completeness of
their program records within PHXYou.

This policy applies to Emergency Medical Services (EMS), Aircraft Rescue and Firefighting (ARFF),
Hazardous Materials Technician (HMT), Technical Rescue Technician (TRT), and other functional
training programs designated by the Department.

Use of PHXYou does not establish a single centralized section responsible for maintaining all
Department training records. Each functional section remains responsible for its own program
administration, record maintenance, compliance monitoring, and audits. As Phase 3 functionality and
additional PHXYou capabilities are implemented, the Department will evaluate opportunities to
improve coordination, standardization, reporting, and Department-wide visibility of training records.

PHXYou Training Records
To the extent supported by system functionality, functional sections shall use PHXYou to document
and maintain applicable program records, including:
 • Initial, recurrent, and required functional training.
 • Continuing education courses, attendance, and accumulated CE hours.
 • Member certifications, qualifications, and certification expiration dates.
 • Required assessments, testing, competency, or proficiency evaluations when supported by the system.
 • Approved outside training used to satisfy Department or program requirements.

• Other training or compliance records required by the applicable functional program.

Functional sections may use supplemental Department-approved systems or files when PHXYou does
not support a specific program requirement. Supplemental systems shall not replace required PHXYou
documentation when the applicable information can reasonably be maintained in PHXYou.


Functional Section Responsibilities
Each functional section shall designate appropriate program managers, training officers, instructors, or
administrative personnel to maintain its training program and records within PHXYou. Each section is
responsible for:
 • Establishing and maintaining applicable courses, curricula, certifications, and training requirements in
    PHXYou.
 • Ensuring required training and CE completions are accurately documented.
 • Maintaining certification and expiration information when supported by PHXYou.
 • Monitoring members for completion of required training, CE, certifications, and proficiency
    requirements.
 • Reviewing records for accuracy and correcting identified discrepancies.
 • Conducting periodic audits of member training and certification records.
 • Identifying and notifying members of training, certification, or CE deficiencies.
 • Coordinating make-up training, remediation, or other corrective action required by the applicable
    program.
 • Maintaining supplemental documentation when a record cannot be fully maintained within PHXYou.


Emergency Medical Services
The EMS Section shall use PHXYou to document applicable Department provided EMS training and
continuing education and, where supported, maintain or track certification information for EMT and
Paramedic members. The EMS Section remains responsible for monitoring certification requirements,
CE completion, CORE training, and other applicable EMS training requirements.

Members completing approved outside continuing education are responsible for submitting required
documentation to the EMS Section. Records required to support Arizona Department of Health
Services audits, recertification, medical direction, or other regulatory requirements may also be
maintained in supplemental Department-approved systems or files when necessary.

Aircraft Rescue and Firefighting
The Fire Aviation Section shall use PHXYou to administer and document applicable ARFF training
requirements, including assigned recurrent training and other training or certification requirements
supported by the system.

The ARFF Training Officer and Fire Aviation Section remain responsible for monitoring member
compliance with initial and recurrent training, live-fire requirements, annual check rides, proficiency
evaluations, Engineer Continuing Education, and other applicable ARFF program requirements.

Hazardous Materials and Technical Rescue
Special Operations shall use PHXYou to document and track applicable HMT and TRT training and
continuing education. PHXYou shall be used to maintain applicable CE hours and training records and
to provide members and program management access to accumulated training information.

Special Operations may continue to use Power Apps, SharePoint, or other approved systems for
program administration and documentation that cannot currently be maintained within PHXYou.
Program Managers, Lead Instructors, and the Special Operations Labor/Management Team remain
responsible for establishing annual CE requirements, evaluating technician competency, monitoring
participation, conducting required audits, and addressing identified deficiencies.

Member Responsibilities
Members participating in functional training programs are responsible for:

• Completing assigned training and CE by established deadlines.
• Monitoring their PHXYou training record for accuracy and completeness.
• Maintaining required certifications, qualifications, and program requirements.
• Providing documentation for approved outside training or certifications when required.
• Notifying the appropriate functional section of missing, inaccurate, or incomplete PHXYou records.
• Promptly addressing identified training, certification, or CE deficiencies.

Use of PHXYou does not relieve individual members of responsibility for maintaining the
certifications, qualifications, CE requirements, or other conditions required for their assignment or
position.

Training Record Audits
Each functional section shall establish a process for periodically auditing its PHXYou training and
certification records. Audit frequency may vary based on program requirements, certification cycles,
regulatory requirements, or operational need.

Audits should verify, as applicable:

• Required training and CE have been completed and accurately recorded.
• Required certifications and qualifications are current.
•   Certification expiration dates are accurate.
•   Required assessments, competencies, or proficiency evaluations have been completed.
•   Approved outside training has been appropriately reviewed and documented.
•   Previously identified deficiencies have been corrected or are being addressed.

Sections should conduct audits with sufficient frequency to identify and address deficiencies before a
member reaches a training, CE, certification, or program compliance deadline.

Record Discrepancies and Deficiencies
When a training or certification record is missing, incomplete, or inaccurate, the responsible functional
section shall review available documentation and make the appropriate correction in PHXYou. When
an audit identifies that a member has not completed a required training, CE, certification, assessment,
or proficiency requirement, the functional section shall follow the notification and remediation process
established by the applicable program.

Supplemental Records
PHXYou is the primary Department system for applicable training and certification records; however,
specialized programs may maintain supplemental records when PHXYou does not support the required
record type or when additional documentation is required for regulatory, certification, confidentiality,
or program purposes.

Supplemental records shall be maintained in an approved Department or City system and in
accordance with applicable records retention requirements.

Phase 3 And Future Phxyou Functionality
The Department will continue coordinating with the PHXYou team as Phase 3 functionality and additional
system capabilities are implemented. As new functionality becomes available, the Department will
evaluate opportunities to expand certification and expiration tracking, improve automated compliance
notifications, standardize reporting, reduce duplicate recordkeeping, improve Department-level visibility,
and transition appropriate records from supplemental systems into PHXYou.

Records Retention
Training and certification records shall be retained in accordance with applicable City of Phoenix and
Phoenix Fire Department records retention requirements and any regulatory or program specific
requirements. Records requiring retention outside PHXYou shall be maintained by the responsible
functional section in an approved Department or City repository.

Responsibilities
Functional Section Leadership and Program Managers are responsible for administering their respective
training programs, maintaining accurate PHXYou records, monitoring compliance, conducting audits, and
addressing identified deficiencies.

Training Officers, Lead Instructors, and designated program staff are responsible for documenting
applicable training, CE, assessments, and program requirements and ensuring records are entered into
PHXYou in a timely and accurate manner.

Members are responsible for completing required training, maintaining required certifications and
qualifications, monitoring their PHXYou records, submitting required outside training documentation,
and promptly addressing identified discrepancies or deficiencies.

Department Leadership is responsible for establishing consistent expectations for PHXYou use across
functional areas and coordinating future system functionality that supports Department-wide training
record management, reporting, and oversight.
